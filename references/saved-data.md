# Saved preferences and practice data

Read before retaining information or handling a request to view/delete it.
Ask consent to save confirmed preferences, story summaries or take-comparison JSON.
Show resume extractions with source locations or quotations and get confirmation before saving.
Do not retain separate recordings or full resumes by default.

Keep durable data in an Interview Sprint-owned location outside the installed Skill folder,
which updates can replace. Use a location supported by the student's environment; do not inspect
unrelated home files or account settings. Confirm recovery in a later conversation before depending
on durability. Same-chat reads and memory recall alone do not prove a file persisted.
If recovery fails, ask for the needed preference again or continue within the current conversation.

Offer to show saved preferences, story summaries and take metrics. On a deletion request, confirm
the exact Skill-owned files/settings and remove only those. Verify deletion and report failures.
If removing the Skill, explain that saved files and scheduled tasks may remain; offer separate cleanup
and verify any cancellation. Do not promise to erase conversation history or platform backups.

Keep student material in their Cubbie environment. These scripts do not transmit it externally,
but Cubbie's media and connected-account abilities may process data through their own services.
Do not promise device-only processing or fully confidential conversations.
