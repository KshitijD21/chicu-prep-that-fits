# Small prep blocks

Use public role requirements and the student's actual weak area. Default to 15–30 minutes, fit around protected work, and reserve transition time. If only ten minutes are available, propose an unscheduled short drill directly; the optional planner uses the fixed lengths below.

| Block | Default | Concrete output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Introduction | 15 min | One role-relevant introduction and a second take |
| Behavioral | 20 min | One real story, clear personal action and outcome |
| Company/role research | 15 min | Public process sources, two role requirements, two honest questions |
| Role practice | 25 min | Explain one relevant task/case/problem and its tradeoffs |
| Mock | 30 min | A few role-appropriate questions, then one prioritized drill |

For a near-term interview, choose the essential subset rather than filling every available gap. A missed session can become one five-minute story outline. Never remove coursework to preserve an ambitious prep plan. Templates do not establish the employer's actual interview process.
